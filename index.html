How we work

From a question to a running portfolio.

  1. Understand

    What you need to achieve, where, by when, and what it must cost. We put the question in writing before we answer it.

  2. Plan

    Locations, properties and funding routes, ranked against your criteria, with the incentives each one unlocks.

  3. Secure

    We negotiate the property and the incentive package together, because each one strengthens the other.

  4. Run

    We manage the portfolio, carry out the upgrades, and keep the compliance reporting that protects your incentives.
