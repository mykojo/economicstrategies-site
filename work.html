A components manufacturer moves production to Gauteng

A European manufacturer needed a plant closer to its Southern African customers. We ranked three provinces, found an unadvertised site inside a Special Economic Zone, negotiated a long lease, and secured an incentive package tied to the jobs created in the first three years.

A logistics operator upgrades six Western Cape warehouses

Rising energy costs were eroding margins across a portfolio of six warehouses. We planned a phased energy and roofing upgrade, procured the contractors, and matched each phase to the grant support available, so the programme funded part of itself.
