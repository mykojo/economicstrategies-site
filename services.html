Services

Every decision about place, in one team.

Strategy sets the direction. Property gives it an address. Portfolio management keeps it earning. Incentives pay for part of it. We do all four, and we do them in that order.

01 Strategy

Where to grow, and why there.

We assess markets and locations against what your organisation actually needs: customers, labour, logistics, cost and the public funding available. The output is a business case your board can approve and a shortlist your property team can act on.

  • Market and location assessment
  • Operating model and cost base
  • Business case and funding route
  • Board and investor papers
02 Property

The right site, on the right terms.

We search the market, including sites that are not advertised, appraise each option technically and commercially, and negotiate the purchase or lease. Where a Special Economic Zone or incentive scheme changes the economics, we build that into the terms from the start.

  • Site and building search
  • Technical and commercial appraisal
  • Negotiation and acquisition
  • Lease and purchase structuring
03 Portfolio management

Run it well. Upgrade it when it pays.

We manage the portfolio day to day and plan the upgrades that raise its value: refurbishment, energy and water efficiency, compliance and occupier fit. Every upgrade is tested against the grant and incentive support available before a contractor is appointed.

  • Asset and occupier management
  • Refurbishment and energy upgrades
  • Contractor procurement and oversight
  • Value and performance reporting
04 Incentives and job creation

Public funding tied to the jobs you create.

Governments and development agencies pay for jobs, investment and skills. We map the grants, subsidies and tax incentives you qualify for, prepare the applications, negotiate with the agencies, and run the compliance reporting that keeps the money secured.

  • Incentive and grant mapping
  • Applications and agency negotiation
  • Jobs and investment compliance
  • Annual reporting and audit support
